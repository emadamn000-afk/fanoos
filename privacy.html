Fanoos – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Fanoos is a private tool operated by Emad Amin Anwar to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels («حكايات الفانوس» and «Fanoos Tales») using the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

With the channel owner's authorization, Fanoos uses Google OAuth scopes to upload videos and manage video metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, scheduling) on the owner's channels only. It does not access, collect or store data about other YouTube users.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored securely as private environment variables and are used only to perform uploads. No personal data is sold or shared with third parties.

YouTube and Google

Fanoos uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
