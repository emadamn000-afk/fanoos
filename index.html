YouTube Fanoos uses YouTube API Services.

Fanoos is a small private tool used to upload our own story videos to our own YouTube channels, «حكايات الفانوس» (Arabic) and «Fanoos Tales» (English), through the YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
