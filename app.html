Fanoos Publisher

Publishes our own short videos to our own TikTok accounts

1Connect a TikTok account

Sign in with TikTok to let Fanoos Publisher post videos to your account. Scopes: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish.

Owner settings (stored only in this browser)

2Choose a video and post settings

Connect an account first.

By posting, you agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.

3Publish

"Post to TikTok" publishes directly (video.publish). "Send as draft" puts the video in your TikTok inbox to finish in the app (video.upload). It can take a few minutes for TikTok to process the video.
